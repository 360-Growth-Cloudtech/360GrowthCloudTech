## 1. Executive Summary & Methodology

### 1.1 Data Source & Extraction Standards
The keyword strategy for 360CloudTech is derived from 18 Google Keyword Planner CSV exports spanning a full 12-month trailing cycle (1 November 2025 to 31 October 2026). Over 29,950 total keyword data points across the United States, United Kingdom, and India were extracted, normalized, and classified into commercial intent clusters.

- **Geographic Segmentation:** Each keyword record preserves its explicit country dataset mapping (USA, UK, India). Search volumes reflect local monthly query velocity in that specific territory.
- **Reporting Currency Disclosure:** Bid metrics (Low Top-of-Page Bid and High Top-of-Page Bid) in the source files are denominated in INR (Indian Rupee, ₹), reflecting the exporting Google Ads billing currency. These are preserved verbatim to provide accurate commercial CPC intent indicators without currency conversion distortion.
- **Competition vs Organic Difficulty:** Google Ads "Competition" (Low, Medium, High) and "Competition (Indexed Value)" (0–100) indicate advertiser commercial bidding intensity. In this mapping, high indexed competition combined with substantial CPC bid ranges signals high-intent transactional buyer demand.
- **B2B Service Intent Filtering:** Informational queries (e.g., tutorial queries, free code snippets, "how to code", salary/career searches) have been filtered out. Only commercial and transactional queries seeking software engineering companies, agency partners, custom development services, and outsourcing squads are mapped.

### 1.2 Multi-Region Single Canonical Architecture
Per business directives, **no separate geo-subfolder doorway pages** (e.g., `/usa/`, `/uk/`, `/india/`) have been created. Instead, each core service is anchored to a single authoritative canonical URL. Each page serves US, UK, and Indian enterprise clients simultaneously through:
1. **Reciprocal Hreflang Alternates:** Annotating `en-US`, `en-GB`, `en-IN`, and `x-default` to the same canonical URL.
2. **Dual-Shore Value Proposition:** US & UK business-hour overlap, local technical leadership, NDA protection, and 100% intellectual property ownership paired with high-velocity engineering delivery.
3. **Structured Data Localization:** Organization and Service schema explicitly defining `areaServed` as United States, United Kingdom, and India.
